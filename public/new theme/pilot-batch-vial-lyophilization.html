Our Core Service

Small-Batch, Documented Lyophilization in Glass Vials

Once your formulation and cycle are locked, we scale production into glass vials — sized for R&D use, validation studies, product launches, and early commercial supply, without the commitment of large-scale manufacturing.

What This Service Includes

Vial Format Selection

We match glass vial size and format to your product, fill volume, and downstream use.

Small & Mid-Size Batch Runs

Batches sized for R&D quantities, validation studies, and launch-stage supply — not full commercial volumes.

Run-to-Run Consistency

Every batch follows the same locked cycle and is checked against the same specifications, so results don't drift between runs.

Batch-Level Testing on Request

A completed batch can be sent to an independent, accredited lab for sterility or other testing, with results shared directly with you.

Why This Matters for Your Project

  • You get production-ready vials without building or staffing an in-house freeze-dryer.
  • Small batch sizes mean less product tied up in a single run — useful for validation and early-launch stages.
  • Every run is documented, so a batch made this month behaves the same as one made next quarter.

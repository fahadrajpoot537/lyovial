Freeze-dried reagent contract manufacturing

Lyolab Diagnostics specializes in freeze-dried reagent contract manufacturing, including lyophilized vial fill and finish — the step where formulation becomes a stable, shippable product.

RUO active peptides wholesale

Beyond contract manufacturing, Lyolab supplies research-use-only active peptides at bulk and wholesale volume for laboratory and research customers, from single labs to CROs and diagnostic developers sourcing material at scale.

Intended use All RUO peptide material is supplied strictly for laboratory research use. It is not intended for human or veterinary use, diagnostic use, or any clinical application.
  • Wholesale and bulk RUO peptide supply in vials
  • Research-use-only active peptides for laboratory customers
  • Sourcing for labs, CROs, and diagnostic developers needing volume pricing
